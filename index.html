Privacy Policy for Magbot: Climb Escape

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Minerva Studios ("we", "our", or "us") operates Magbot: Climb Escape (the "Game").

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, processed, and shared when you use Magbot: Climb Escape on Android or iOS.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Magbot: Climb Escape does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, postal address, or phone number.

However, the Game uses third-party services that may automatically collect or process certain information from your device.

Depending on your device, operating system, region, permissions, consent choices, and the services being used, this information may include:

  • Device type and device model
  • Operating system and operating system version
  • App version
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers where permitted
  • IP address
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address or network information
  • App interactions and gameplay events
  • Session information
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Advertising impressions and interactions
  • Push-notification registration tokens and related technical information
  • Other technical information required to operate, secure, analyze, advertise, and improve the Game

2. Google Firebase

Magbot: Climb Escape uses services provided by Google Firebase to help operate, analyze, monitor, and improve the Game.

2.1 Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with the Game.

Firebase Analytics may process information relating to:

  • App launches
  • Gameplay sessions
  • Game events
  • Feature usage
  • Engagement information
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • App version
  • General usage information

We use this information to understand how the Game is used, measure engagement, identify areas for improvement, and improve the overall player experience.

2.2 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, errors, and stability issues.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

  • Crash logs
  • Stack traces
  • Device information
  • Operating system information
  • App state
  • App version
  • Technical identifiers
  • Diagnostic information

This information is used to diagnose technical problems and improve the stability and reliability of the Game.

2.3 Firebase Cloud Messaging

We may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send push notifications related to Magbot: Climb Escape.

Firebase, Google, Apple, and the device operating system may process technical information necessary to deliver notifications, including push notification registration tokens.

You may disable push notifications at any time through your device settings.

3. Advertising

Magbot: Climb Escape uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

The Game may include rewarded advertisements and other advertising formats that may be added or changed over time.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information including:

  • Advertising identifiers

  • Device information
  • Operating system information

  • Ad interactions

  • Fraud-prevention information
  • Advertising measurement information

Depending on your region, age eligibility, device settings, consent choices, and applicable laws, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise restricted.

Depending on your location, you may be presented with options relating to:

  • Personalized advertising
